Add your take

For each moment, write one or two sentences of your own: context, a consequence, or your view. That's what makes a clip yours (and what YouTube's reused-content policy asks for). fair-clip never writes it for you.

Posting them

  1. YouTube Studio → Create → Upload videos → pick a clip.
  2. Paste its title and description (the Copy buttons above).
  3. Tick what its checklist says, especially Altered or synthetic content.
  4. Upload as Private, watch it once on your phone, then publish.
